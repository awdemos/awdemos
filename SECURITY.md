# Security policy

Security issues in this repository are handled privately. Do **not** open a
public issue, discussion, or pull request for a vulnerability.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email [contact@vibecodingagency.com](mailto:contact@vibecodingagency.com) with
the subject line `SECURITY: awdemos`. Include:

- the affected file, demo, or deployment path;
- a concrete reproduction (commands, inputs, or a minimal script);
- the impact you believe is possible; and
- your preferred contact method for follow-up.

You should receive an acknowledgement within five business days. I will work
with you on a fix and coordinates disclosure. Please allow **90 days** before
any public disclosure so a verified fix and advisory can be prepared.

## Scope

- Source code, scripts, container images, and deployment manifests in this
  repository.
- The public sites and standards linked from the README where the issue is
  caused by content in this repository.

Out of scope: third-party services not controlled by this repository, and
issues that require social engineering against maintainers.

## Safe-harbor expectations

Good-faith research that avoids data destruction, service disruption, and
access to unrelated systems is welcome. Do not test against live production
systems belonging to third parties.

## Repository rules

- Never commit credentials, tokens, keys, or real customer data.
- Prefer private reporting with a 90-day embargo over public issue disclosure.
- Signed commits are required for contributions; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
